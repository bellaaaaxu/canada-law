## 法律原文

> **Section 35(1):** An employer must pay an employee overtime wages in accordance with section 40 if the employer requires, or directly or indirectly allows, the employee to work more than **8 hours a day** or 40 hours a week.

> **Section 40(1):** An employer must pay an employee who works over 8 hours a day, and is not working under an averaging agreement under section 37,
> - (a) **1½ times** the employee's regular wage for the time over **8 hours**; and
> - (b) **double** the employee's regular wage for any time over **12 hours**.

## 什么意思

在BC，一天工作超过 **8 小时**就要付加班费：

| 每日工作时长 | 工资倍率 |
|---|---|
| 前 8 小时 | 正常工资 |
| 超过 8 小时、不超过 12 小时的部分 | **1.5 倍**正常工资 |
| 超过 12 小时的部分 | **2 倍**正常工资 |

此外，一周工作超过 **40 小时**也要按 1.5 倍付加班费（计算周加班时，每天只算前 8 小时）。

## 影响结果的因素

- 是否签有**平均工时协议**（averaging agreement, s.37）——如果有，加班门槛会不同。
- 员工是否属于法规豁免或特殊规定的行业（如油气田工人等）。
- 工作场所是**省级管辖**还是**联邦管辖**（银行、航空、电信、跨省运输等属联邦，适用不同法律，本查询不涵盖）。

## What the law says

> **Section 35(1):** "An employer must pay an employee overtime wages in accordance with section 40 if the employer requires, or directly or indirectly allows, the employee to work more than **8 hours a day** or **40 hours a week**."

> **Section 40(1):** "An employer must pay an employee who works over 8 hours a day…
> (a) **1½ times** the employee's regular wage for the time over 8 hours, and
> (b) **double** the employee's regular wage for any time over 12 hours."

> **Section 40(2):** "An employer must pay an employee who works over 40 hours a week… 1½ times the employee's regular wage for the time over 40 hours."

## What it means

In BC, overtime pay kicks in after **8 hours in a day**:

- **Hours 8–12:** paid at **1.5× (time and a half)** your regular wage.
- **Beyond 12 hours:** paid at **2× (double time)** your regular wage.

There is also weekly overtime: if you work more than **40 hours in a week**, the hours beyond 40 are paid at 1.5× (only the first 8 hours of each day count toward the weekly total).

## What decides the outcome

- Whether the employee is working under an **averaging agreement** (s. 37) — if so, different rules apply instead.
- Whether the employee is in an occupation **excluded** from overtime under the Employment Standards Regulation (e.g., certain managers, professionals, or specific industries like oil and gas field workers).
- Whether the workplace is **federally regulated** (banks, airlines, telecommunications, interprovincial transport, etc.) — those fall under federal law, not BC's *Employment Standards Act*.

## What the law says

The *Employment Standards Act* (RSBC 1996, c. 113), section 1(1) defines **"statutory holiday"** as:

> New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, British Columbia Day, Labour Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, Thanksgiving Day, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day and any other holiday prescribed by regulation.

## What it means

BC has **11 statutory holidays**:

2. **Family Day** – 3rd Monday in February
3. **Good Friday** – Friday before Easter Sunday
4. **Victoria Day** – Monday before May 25
5. **Canada Day** – July 1

Here's what BC law says about compensation when you lose your job.

---

## What the law says

Under **section 63** of the *Employment Standards Act*, after 12 consecutive months of employment an employer becomes liable to pay **compensation for length of service** equal to **2 weeks' wages**:

> *"(2) The employer's liability for compensation for length of service increases as follows:
> (a) after 12 consecutive months of employment, to an amount equal to 2 weeks' wages"*

The employer can discharge this liability by giving the equivalent **written notice** (2 weeks' notice for 12+ months of service), a **combination** of notice and pay, or if the employee quit, retired, or was dismissed for **just cause** (s. 63(3)).

The pay is calculated based on the employee's regular wage over the last 8 weeks of normal/average hours worked (s. 63(4)).

Also relevant: if a layoff exceeds a "temporary layoff" (generally 13 weeks in a 20-week period), the employment is **deemed terminated** at the start of the layoff (s. 63(5)), meaning compensation for length of service would be owed.

## What it means

In BC, "severance" is called **compensation for length of service**. An employee with 2 years of consecutive service falls into the "after 12 consecutive months" bracket, which means the employer owes the equivalent of **2 weeks' wages** — unless the employer gave proper written notice or a combination of notice and pay instead, or the employee was dismissed for just cause.

## What decides the outcome

The key facts that determine how this applies in a specific case include:

- **Length of consecutive service** — exactly how long the employment lasted
- **Whether written notice of termination was given**, and how much
- **Whether the termination was for just cause**
- **Whether the employee quit or retired**
- **Whether this is a layoff** — and if so, whether it exceeds a "temporary layoff" (which would trigger deemed termination)
- **The employee's regular wage** over the last 8 weeks of normal hours

## 法律怎么说

根据 BC 省《Employment Standards Act》第 63 条：

> **63 (2)** The employer's liability for compensation for length of service increases as follows:
> (a) after 12 consecutive months of employment, to an amount equal to **2 weeks' wages**;
> (b) after 3 consecutive years of employment, to an amount equal to 3 weeks' wages …

> **63 (3)** The liability is deemed to be discharged if the employee
> (a) is given **written notice of termination** as follows: …
> (ii) **2 weeks' notice** after 12 consecutive months of employment; …
> (b) is given a **combination** of written notice under subsection (3)(a) and money equivalent to the amount the employer is liable to pay …

## 这意味着什么

连续工作超过 12 个月但不满 3 年的员工，如果被无故解雇（不是因为员工自身过错），雇主必须：

- 提前给 **2 周的书面通知**，或者
- 支付相当于 **2 周工资**的补偿金，或者
- 以上两者的组合（比如 1 周通知 + 1 周工资补偿）。

2 周工资的计算方式是：取最后正常工作的 8 周的工资总额，除以 8，再乘以 2。

## 决定结果的关键因素

以下因素会影响你的实际情况：

- **连续工作时间**：是否确实满了 12 个月以上、不满 3 年。
- **解雇原因**：如果雇主认定是"just cause"（正当理由解雇），则无需支付补偿（第 63(3)(c) 条）。
- **是否已给通知**：雇主是否已经提前给了书面通知，或者给了部分通知加部分补偿金。
- **行业性质**：大多数温哥华餐馆受 BC 省法律管辖，但如果雇主属于联邦监管行业（银行、航空等），则适用联邦法律，本查询不涵盖联邦法律。
