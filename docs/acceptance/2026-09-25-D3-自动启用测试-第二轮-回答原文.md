## 法律原文

> **Section 35(1):** An employer must pay an employee overtime wages in accordance with section 40 if the employer requires, or directly or indirectly allows, the employee to work more than **8 hours a day** or **40 hours a week**.

> **Section 40(1):** An employer must pay an employee who works over 8 hours a day, and is not working under an averaging agreement under section 37,
> (a) **1 1/2 times** the employee's regular wage for the time over 8 hours, and
> (b) **double** the employee's regular wage for any time over 12 hours.

> **Section 40(2):** An employer must pay an employee who works over 40 hours a week … 1 1/2 times the employee's regular wage for the time over 40 hours.

## 简单解释

在 BC，**一天工作超过 8 小时**就要付加班费：

| 每日工作时数 | 工资倍率 |
|---|---|
| 前 8 小时 | 正常工资 |
| 超过 8 小时至 12 小时 | **1.5 倍**正常工资 |
| 超过 12 小时 | **2 倍**正常工资 |

此外，**一周超过 40 小时**也触发加班费（1.5 倍），但计算周加班时每天只算前 8 小时。

## 影响结果的关键因素

- **是否有"平均工时协议"（averaging agreement）**：如果雇主和员工签订了 s.37 的平均工时协议，上述门槛会不同。
- **行业类别**：某些行业（如油气田工人）有特殊规定。某些职位可能被排除在加班要求之外（见 Employment Standards Regulation s.34）。
- **联邦管辖行业**：银行、航空、电信、跨省运输等属于联邦法律管辖，不适用 BC 省法，请确认您的雇主属于哪种管辖。

https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/contact-us

## What the law says

> "An employer must pay an employee who works over **8 hours a day**, and is not working under an averaging agreement under section 37,
> (a) **1½ times** the employee's regular wage for the time over 8 hours, and
> (b) **double** the employee's regular wage for any time over 12 hours."
>
> — *Employment Standards Act*, s. 40(1)

There is also weekly overtime:

> "An employer must pay an employee who works over **40 hours a week** … 1½ times the employee's regular wage for the time over 40 hours."
>
> — *Employment Standards Act*, s. 40(2)

## What it means

In BC, overtime pay kicks in after **8 hours in a day**:

- Hours 8–12 in a day are paid at **1.5× regular wage**.
- Hours beyond 12 in a day are paid at **double** the regular wage.
- Hours beyond 40 in a week (counting only the first 8 hours of each day) are paid at 1.5×.

## What decides the outcome

- Whether the employee is working under an **averaging agreement** (s. 37) — if so, different rules apply.
- Whether the employee falls under a **special regulation** (e.g., oil and gas field workers have separate overtime rules).
- Whether the workplace is **provincially or federally regulated** — federally regulated industries (banks, airlines, telecommunications, interprovincial transport, etc.) fall under federal law, not the BC *Employment Standards Act*.

## What the law says

> New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, British Columbia Day, Labour Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, Thanksgiving Day, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day and any other holiday prescribed by regulation.

## What it means

BC has **11 statutory holidays**:

2. **Family Day** — third Monday in February

4. **Victoria Day** — Monday before May 25
5. **Canada Day** — July 1

### What the law says

> **Section 63(1)–(2)** of the *Employment Standards Act*:
>
> "After 3 consecutive months of employment, the employer becomes liable to pay an employee an amount equal to one week's wages as compensation for length of service."
>
> "The employer's liability for compensation for length of service increases as follows: (a) after 12 consecutive months of employment, to an amount equal to **2 weeks' wages**…"

The employer can discharge this liability by giving the equivalent **written notice of termination** (2 weeks' notice for 12+ months of employment), or a combination of notice and pay (s. 63(3)).

### What it means

Under BC law, the concept is called **"compensation for length of service"** (often called severance or termination pay). After 2 years of consecutive employment, your employer's minimum obligation is **2 weeks' wages** — either as termination pay, as 2 weeks' written notice, or a combination of both. The amount increases to 3 weeks after 3 years, and grows from there.

### What decides the outcome

The key facts that determine how this applies are:

- **Length of consecutive employment** — at 2 years, the statutory minimum is 2 weeks' wages.
- **Whether written notice was given** — if the employer gave you proper advance notice, that can reduce or eliminate the pay owed.
- **Whether a combination of notice and pay was used** — the employer can split the obligation between the two.
- **Whether there was just cause for dismissal** — if an employee is dismissed for just cause, no compensation is owed (s. 63(3)(c)). A layoff is not just cause.
- **How "weekly wages" are calculated** — the Act specifies averaging the last 8 weeks of regular wages (s. 63(4)).

---

## 法律原文（英文）

> **Section 63(2)(a):**
> "After 12 consecutive months of employment, [the employer's liability increases] to an amount equal to **2 weeks' wages**."
>
> **Section 63(3):** The liability is deemed to be discharged if the employee is given **written notice of termination** (2 weeks' notice after 12 consecutive months), or a combination of written notice and money equivalent to the amount owed.

---

## 这意味着什么

在BC省，雇主解雇员工（无正当理由）时，需要根据员工的**连续工作时长**支付"遣散补偿"（compensation for length of service）。你工作了**两年**（超过12个月但不满3年），按法律规定，雇主需要：

- 支付相当于 **2 周工资** 的补偿，**或者**
- 提前给你 **2 周的书面解雇通知**，**或者**
- 以上两者的组合（比如1周通知 + 1周工资）

## 决定结果的关键因素

- **连续工作时间**：必须是连续受雇，中间是否有超过临时裁员时限的中断会影响计算。
- **解雇原因**：如果雇主能证明是"正当理由"（just cause）解雇，则无需支付补偿。你说的是"被裁了"，通常属于无正当理由的终止雇佣。
- **2周工资的计算方式**：按你最后8个正常工作周的工资总和除以8，再乘以2（见第63(4)条）。
- **是否已给书面通知**：如果雇主已经提前给了足够的书面通知，则可以不另付补偿金。

## 请注意

- 以上是BC省法律下的**最低标准**。根据普通法（common law），你可能有权获得更多的合理通知期补偿，具体取决于你的年龄、职位、工作年限等因素。
- 大多数温哥华的餐馆属于BC省管辖，但如果是联邦监管行业（如银行、航空等），则适用联邦法律，本技能暂不覆盖。
