根据BC省《Employment Standards Act》的规定：

---

### 1. 法律原文

> **Section 40(1):** An employer must pay an employee who works over 8 hours a day, and is not working under an averaging agreement under section 37,
> (a) 1½ times the employee's regular wage for the time over 8 hours, and
> (b) double the employee's regular wage for any time over 12 hours.

### 2. 含义

一天工作**超过 8 小时**就要付加班费：

- **8–12 小时**的部分：按正常工资的 **1.5 倍**计算
- **超过 12 小时**的部分：按正常工资的 **2 倍**计算

此外，一周工作超过 40 小时也需要支付 1.5 倍加班费（按每天前 8 小时累计计算）。

### 3. 影响结果的关键因素

- 雇员是否签署了**工时平均协议**（averaging agreement, s.37）——如有，加班计算方式不同
- 雇员是否属于该法律适用范围（联邦监管行业如银行、航空、电信等适用联邦法律，不在此列）
- 具体的工作时间记录

## What the law says

> "An employer must pay an employee overtime wages … if the employer requires, or directly or indirectly allows, the employee to work **more than 8 hours a day** or 40 hours a week."
> — *Employment Standards Act*, s. 35(1)

The overtime rates are set out in s. 40:

> - **1½ times** the regular wage for time over **8 hours** in a day
> - **Double** the regular wage for time over **12 hours** in a day
> - **1½ times** the regular wage for time over **40 hours** in a week

## What it means

Daily overtime in BC kicks in after **8 hours of work in a day**. You get time-and-a-half for hours 9 through 12, and double time for anything beyond 12 hours. Separately, weekly overtime (time-and-a-half) applies after 40 hours in a week, counting only the first 8 hours of each day toward that weekly total.

## What decides the outcome

- Whether the employee is covered by the BC *Employment Standards Act* (federally regulated workers are not — see note below).
- Whether the employee is working under an **averaging agreement** (s. 37), which changes how overtime is calculated.
- Whether the employee falls into an occupation excluded or varied by regulation.

**Federal note:** Workers in federally regulated industries (banks, airlines, telecommunications, interprovincial transport, etc.) fall under federal labour law, not the BC *Employment Standards Act*. This answer covers BC provincial law only.

## 法律原文

> **32 (1)** An employer must ensure
> (a) that no employee works more than **5 consecutive hours** without a meal break, and
> (b) that each meal break lasts at least a **1/2 hour**.
>
> **(2)** An employer who requires an employee to work or be available for work during a meal break must count the meal break as time worked by the employee.

## 含义

BC 的《就业标准法》规定，雇主必须确保员工**连续工作不超过 5 小时**就要给一次餐休，且每次餐休**至少 30 分钟**。如果雇主要求员工在餐休期间工作或待命，则该餐休时间必须算作工作时间并支付工资。

## 决定适用情况的因素

- 员工是否连续工作了超过 5 小时而未获得餐休。
- 餐休期间员工是否被要求工作或待命（如果是，则餐休算作带薪工作时间）。
- 该工作场所是否属于 BC 省管辖（联邦监管行业如银行、航空、电信、跨省运输等适用联邦法律，本技能暂不涵盖）。

## What the law says

> **32 (1)** An employer must ensure
> (a) that no employee works more than **5 consecutive hours** without a meal break, and
> (b) that each meal break lasts at least a **1/2 hour**.
>
> **(2)** An employer who requires an employee to work or be available for work during a meal break must count the meal break as time worked by the employee.

## What it means

Your employer must give you a meal break of at least 30 minutes before you've worked 5 consecutive hours. If your employer makes you work or stay available during that break, they have to pay you for it — it counts as time worked.

## What decides the outcome

- Whether you have worked **5 consecutive hours** without a break.
- Whether your employer requires you to **work or be available** during the meal break (which determines whether it's paid time).
- Whether your workplace is **provincially regulated**. Federally regulated industries (banks, airlines, telecommunications, interprovincial transport, etc.) fall under federal law, not the BC *Employment Standards Act*.
