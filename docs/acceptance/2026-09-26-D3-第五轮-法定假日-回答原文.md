## BC 法定假日 (Statutory Holidays)

### 1. 法律原文

根据 *Employment Standards Act*, RSBC 1996, c. 113, s. 1(1)：

> **"statutory holiday"** means New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, British Columbia Day, Labour Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, Thanksgiving Day, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day and any other holiday prescribed by regulation;

### 2. 含义

BC 的《就业标准法》规定了以下 **11 个法定假日**：

| 法定假日 | 英文名称 |
|---|---|
| 元旦 | New Year's Day |
| 家庭日 | Family Day |
| 耶稣受难日 | Good Friday |
| 维多利亚日 | Victoria Day |
| 加拿大国庆日 | Canada Day |
| 不列颠哥伦比亚日 | British Columbia Day |
| 劳动节 | Labour Day |
| 全国真相与和解日 | National Day for Truth and Reconciliation |
| 感恩节 | Thanksgiving Day |
| 阵亡将士纪念日 | Remembrance Day |
| 圣诞节 | Christmas Day |

此外，法规（regulation）可以额外指定其他法定假日。

### 3. 影响适用的因素

- 员工是否受 BC 省法律管辖（联邦监管行业如银行、航空、电信等适用联邦法律，本技能暂不涵盖）。
- 员工是否在法定假日前至少受雇 30 个日历日，以及是否在前 30 天内工作或赚取了至少 15 天的工资（s. 44），这决定了是否有权获得法定假日薪酬。
- 雇主和员工是否有集体协议，其中关于法定假日的条款可能替代法律要求（但全国真相与和解日除外）。

### 4. 非来自官方文本的补充

以下内容未从官方法律文本中核实：这些法定假日的具体日期每年不同（例如家庭日是二月的第三个星期一，维多利亚日是五月二十五日前的最后一个星期一等）。建议查阅 BC 省政府网站获取当年具体日期。

## What the law says

The *Employment Standards Act*, RSBC 1996, c. 113, section 1(1) defines **"statutory holiday"** as:

> "New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, British Columbia Day, Labour Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, Thanksgiving Day, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day and any other holiday prescribed by regulation"

## What it means

BC has **11 statutory holidays** set out in the Act:

1. **New Year's Day** — January 1
2. **Family Day** — third Monday in February
3. **Good Friday** — the Friday before Easter Sunday
4. **Victoria Day** — the Monday before May 25
5. **Canada Day** — July 1
6. **British Columbia Day** — first Monday in August
7. **Labour Day** — first Monday in September
8. **National Day for Truth and Reconciliation** — September 30
9. **Thanksgiving Day** — second Monday in October
10. **Remembrance Day** — November 11
11. **Christmas Day** — December 25

The definition also allows additional holidays to be added by regulation.

## What decides the outcome

- Whether an employee is **entitled to statutory holiday pay or a day off** depends on meeting the eligibility requirements in section 44 (employed for at least 30 calendar days before the holiday, and worked or earned wages for 15 of the preceding 30 days, or worked under an averaging agreement).
- Some employees may be **excluded** by regulation (e.g., certain categories under the Employment Standards Regulation).
- Collective agreements can replace Part 5 requirements for holidays **other than** the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation (s. 3).

> New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, British Columbia Day, Labour Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, Thanksgiving Day, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day and any other holiday prescribed by regulation.

### What it means

BC has **11 statutory holidays**:

1. **New Year's Day** — January 1

4. **Victoria Day** — Monday before May 25
5. **Canada Day** — July 1

7. **Labour Day** — first Monday in September
8. **National Day for Truth and Reconciliation** — September 30
9. **Thanksgiving Day** — second Monday in October
