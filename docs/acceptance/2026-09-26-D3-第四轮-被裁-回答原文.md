## What the law says

The *Employment Standards Act*, s. 63 provides:

> **63 (1)** After 3 consecutive months of employment, the employer becomes liable to pay an employee an amount equal to one week's wages as compensation for length of service.
>
> **(2)** The employer's liability for compensation for length of service increases as follows:
> (a) after 12 consecutive months of employment, to an amount equal to **2 weeks' wages**;
> (b) after 3 consecutive years of employment, to an amount equal to 3 weeks' wages plus one additional week's wages for each additional year of employment, to a maximum of 8 weeks' wages.
>
> **(3)** The liability is deemed to be discharged if the employee
> (a) is given written notice of termination [matching the same schedule — 2 weeks' notice after 12 consecutive months];
> (b) is given a combination of written notice and money equivalent to the amount the employer is liable to pay; or
> (c) terminates the employment, retires from employment, or **is dismissed for just cause**.

## What it means

Under BC law, when an employer ends your employment (including by layoff), they owe you **compensation for length of service** — what people commonly call "severance." After 2 years (i.e. after 12 consecutive months but before 3 years), the amount is **2 weeks' wages**. The employer can satisfy this obligation by giving you:

- 2 weeks' pay, or
- 2 weeks' written notice, or
- a combination of both (e.g. 1 week's notice + 1 week's pay).

The pay is calculated based on your regular wage averaged over the last 8 weeks you worked normal or average hours (s. 63(4)).

## What decides the outcome

Whether and how much you are owed depends on these facts:

- **Length of consecutive employment** — at 2 years you fall in the "after 12 consecutive months" tier (2 weeks' wages).
- **Whether written notice was given** — if the employer gave you proper written notice, the compensation obligation may already be discharged.
- **Whether there was just cause** — if the employer dismissed you for just cause, no compensation is owed (s. 63(3)(c)).
- **Whether you quit or were laid off** — the obligation arises when the *employer* ends the employment. A layoff beyond a temporary layoff is treated as a termination (s. 63(5)).
- **Whether BC law applies** — most restaurant workers in Vancouver are covered by BC law, but if the employer is federally regulated (unlikely for a restaurant, but possible in rare cases), federal law would apply instead.

## Where to get help

If you believe you are owed compensation, the **Employment Standards Branch** can help you understand your rights and file a complaint:
https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/employment-business/employment-standards-advice/employment-standards/contact-us

## 法律原文（What the law says）

> **63 (1)** After 3 consecutive months of employment, the employer becomes liable to pay an employee an amount equal to one week's wages as compensation for length of service.
>
> **(2)** The employer's liability for compensation for length of service increases as follows:
> (a) after 12 consecutive months of employment, to an amount equal to **2 weeks' wages**;
> (b) after 3 consecutive years of employment, to an amount equal to 3 weeks' wages plus one additional week's wages for each additional year of employment, to a maximum of 8 weeks' wages.
>
> **(3)** The liability is deemed to be discharged if the employee
> (a) is given **written notice of termination** as follows: … (ii) 2 weeks' notice after 12 consecutive months of employment; …
> (b) is given a combination of written notice under subsection (3)(a) and money equivalent to the amount the employer is liable to pay, or
> (c) terminates the employment, retires from employment, or is **dismissed for just cause**.

— *Employment Standards Act*, RSBC 1996, c. 113, s. 63

## 这意味着什么（What it means）

BC 法律管这笔钱叫 **"compensation for length of service"**（按服务年限的补偿），不叫"遣散费"（severance），但效果类似。

连续工作满 **12 个月但不到 3 年**的员工，雇主的法定义务是：

- 支付相当于 **2 周工资**的补偿，**或者**
- 提前 **2 周**给书面解雇通知，**或者**
- 通知和补偿金的组合（加起来等于 2 周）。

如果雇主既没给足通知、也没给钱，就违反了法律。

## 决定结果的关键事实（What decides the outcome）

- **连续工作时间**：你说做了两年。如果确实是连续 12 个月以上、不到 3 年，法定补偿是 2 周工资。
- **解雇原因**：如果雇主以"正当理由"（just cause）解雇你，则没有补偿义务。裁员（layoff）通常不属于 just cause。
- **是否已给通知**：如果雇主提前给了足够的书面通知，就不需要再付钱。
- **工资计算方式**：补偿金按你最后 8 个正常工作周的周工资平均值来算（s. 63(4)）。
- **行业管辖**：餐馆一般受 BC 省法管辖；但如果是联邦管辖行业（银行、航空等），适用的是联邦法律，本回答不涵盖。
