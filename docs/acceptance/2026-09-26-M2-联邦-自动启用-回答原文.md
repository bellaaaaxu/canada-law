## 法律原文

根据《加拿大劳工法》(*Canada Labour Code*) 第166条，**"general holiday"（法定假日）** 的定义如下：

> "general holiday" means **New Year's Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Labour Day, National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, which is observed on September 30, Thanksgiving Day, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day and Boxing Day** and includes any day substituted for any such holiday under section 195;

## 含义

联邦监管行业（如银行、航空、电信、跨省运输等）的雇员享有以下 **10个法定假日**：

1. **元旦** — New Year's Day
2. **耶稣受难日** — Good Friday
3. **维多利亚日** — Victoria Day
4. **加拿大日** — Canada Day
5. **劳动节** — Labour Day
6. **全国真相与和解日** — National Day for Truth and Reconciliation（9月30日）
7. **感恩节** — Thanksgiving Day
8. **国殇纪念日** — Remembrance Day
9. **圣诞节** — Christmas Day
10. **节礼日** — Boxing Day

## 影响结果的因素

- 根据第195条，雇主可以通过集体协议或经雇员投票同意，以其他日期替代上述任何假日。
- 根据第192条，每位雇员在受雇期间有权享受带薪法定假日。
- 根据第196条，假日工资的计算方式取决于雇员在假日前四周的收入。
- 如果雇员在法定假日当天被要求工作，根据第197条，除假日工资外，还需支付不低于正常工资1.5倍的额外报酬。

## 不来自官方文本的内容

以下具体日期未从法律文本中查询，仅供参考：维多利亚日通常在5月25日之前的星期一；加拿大日为7月1日；劳动节为9月第一个星期一；感恩节为10月第二个星期一；国殇纪念日为11月11日。

Since you work for an airline, you are in a **federally regulated** workplace, so the *Canada Labour Code* applies (not BC's *Employment Standards Act*).

---

### What the law says

> **169.1 (1)** Every employee is entitled to and shall be granted an unpaid break of at least 30 minutes during every period of five consecutive hours of work. If the employer requires the employee to be at their disposal during the break period, the employee must be paid for the break.
>
> **(2)** An employer may postpone or cancel the break set out in subsection (1) if it is necessary for the employee to work in order to deal with a situation that the employer could not have reasonably foreseen and that presents or could reasonably be expected to present an imminent or serious (a) threat to the life, health or safety of any person; (b) threat of damage to or loss of property; or (c) threat of serious interference with the ordinary working of the employer's industrial establishment.

### What it means

Your employer must give you an **unpaid 30-minute break before you complete 5 consecutive hours of work**. If you're required to stay available (e.g., on call) during that break, you must be **paid** for it.

The employer can only skip or delay this break in genuine emergencies — unforeseeable situations that threaten life/safety, property, or seriously interfere with operations.

### What decides the outcome

- Whether you have actually worked **5 consecutive hours** without a break.
- Whether your employer requires you to remain **at their disposal** during the break (which would make it paid).
- Whether an emergency exception under s. 169.1(2) applies (the situation must be unforeseeable and pose an imminent or serious threat).
- Whether any modified rules apply to your specific role under the *Exemptions from and Modifications to Hours of Work Provisions Regulations* (SOR/2021-200) — some classes of employees have slightly different wording (e.g., "for every period" vs. "during every period").

### Where to get help

If you believe your employer is not providing the required breaks, you can file a complaint with the **federal Labour Program**:
https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/workplace/federal-labour-standards/filing-complaint.html

**标准工时（第 169(1) 条）：**
> "the standard hours of work of an employee shall not exceed **eight hours in a day** and forty hours in a week"

**加班的定义（第 166 条）：**
> "overtime means **hours of work in excess of standard hours of work**"

**加班费（第 174(1) 条）：**
> "when an employee is required or permitted to work overtime, they are entitled to (a) be paid for the overtime at a rate of wages **not less than one and one-half times** their regular rate of wages; or (b) be granted not less than one and one-half hours of **time off with pay** for each hour of overtime worked"

## 2. 这意味着什么

联邦法律规定，标准工时是**每天 8 小时**、每周 40 小时。超过 8 小时的部分就是加班（overtime）。你每天工作 9 小时，多出的 **1 小时属于加班时间**，雇主应当：

- 按不低于正常时薪的 **1.5 倍**支付加班费；**或者**
- 给你不少于 **1.5 小时的带薪休假**作为补偿（但需要你书面同意）。

- **你是否属于联邦监管行业**——银行通常属于，但请确认你的雇主确实受联邦法律管辖。
- **是否有集体协议（工会合同）或书面协议**改变了工时的计算方式——第 169(2) 条允许在特定情况下对工时做平均计算（averaging），这可能影响加班的认定。
- **你的岗位是否属于豁免类别**——某些管理人员或特定职位可能被排除在加班规定之外（需查看相关规章）。
- **加班是雇主要求还是允许的**——法律要求加班必须是被"required or permitted"（要求或允许）的。

如果你认为雇主没有正确支付加班费，可以向联邦劳工项目（Federal Labour Program）投诉：
https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/workplace/federal-labour-standards/filing-complaint.html

Here is what the law says about your situation.

---

## What the law says

Because your company hauls freight between BC and Alberta, it is engaged in interprovincial transportation and falls under **federal** jurisdiction — the **Canada Labour Code** (Part III) and the **Motor Vehicle Operators Hours of Work Regulations** (C.R.C., c. 990).

Since you drive between provinces and are not a bus operator or a city operator (someone who works exclusively within a 10-mile radius of their home terminal), you are classified as a **"highway motor vehicle operator"** under section 2 of those Regulations.

**Section 6(1)** of the Regulations states:

> "the standard hours of work of a highway motor vehicle operator may exceed 40 hours in a week but **shall not exceed 60 hours**, and no employer shall cause or permit a highway motor vehicle operator to work longer hours than 60 hours in a week."

This replaces the general 48-hour weekly maximum that would otherwise apply under section 171 of the Canada Labour Code.

## What it means

Your employer can schedule you for up to **60 hours of work per week** — not the usual 48-hour cap that applies to most federally regulated employees. They cannot make you work more than 60 hours in a week.

In a week where a general (statutory) holiday falls, the cap drops to **50 hours** (section 6(2)), not counting time worked on the holiday itself.

"Working hours" under these Regulations (section 2) means time from when your shift starts to when you're relieved of duties, **but does not include** time for authorized meals and rest stops, time stopped due to illness or fatigue, time resting in a sleeper berth as a second driver, or time resting at a motel or hotel.

## What decides the outcome

- Whether your employer is actually engaged in **interprovincial** transportation (section 3(d)) — if the freight only moves within BC, federal law may not apply.
- Whether you are a **highway** motor vehicle operator (not a city operator working within a 10-mile radius of your home terminal).
- Whether a **general holiday** falls in a given week (which lowers the cap to 50 hours).
- Whether there is a collective agreement or special authorization that changes the schedule.

## Not from the official text

This part was not checked against the official text: federally regulated interprovincial truck drivers are also subject to the federal **Commercial Vehicle Drivers Hours of Service Regulations** (SOR/2005-313), which impose additional limits on daily driving time, mandatory off-duty periods, and logbook requirements. Those are separate from the weekly hours-of-work cap discussed above.

## Where to get help

If you have concerns about your hours or your employer's compliance, you can contact the **federal Labour Program**: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/jobs/workplace/federal-labour-standards/filing-complaint.html
